================================================================================
1. WHAT WAS WRONG, AND WHAT WAS CHANGED
================================================================================

FINDING 1 - C01 printed cells (province / town / ward / district)
----------------------------------------------------------------
House Number and Street on the same rows already read '2291' and
'Nkululuthweni'. The right-hand cells (and Town) were sitting on the ruling
and clipping the written word. Re-measured the outer cell on c01_p0.png
(1190 x 1684) and inset off the line (inset, not expand).

Left-column Org Household Nr / House Number / Street were left on the Phase 4
boxes because those already read the right text. Insetting them made House
Number regress to 'foc'.

FINDING 2 - C02 alignment AND under-specified atlas
---------------------------------------------------
Both. Confirmed on c02_adult.jpg (754 x 1280 portrait vs landscape blank
1684 x 1190):

  * crop_document used to fail (6-vertex page contour, not a quad).
  * ORB never produced a full-page homography. SIFT found hundreds of inliers
    on one cell of the repeating grid; those warps collapsed to a bowtie and
    were then accepted as C01 with 60 inliers.
  * The NPO blank identity block is 5 value cells. The photographed sheet is
    Version 1/2016 (Known As instead of Org Household Number) and this
    fixture's name/surname/ID block is blank.

Fixes in scan_align.py:

  * SIFT on plain greyscale after ORB, with a projected-quad check (area
    >= 25% of the blank, convex, det in range) so a collapsed match cannot
    win.
  * Prefer a solid ORB page match (>= 70 inliers) when it exists, so C01
    ticks stay on the right boxes.
  * Fallback: rotate to the blank orientation and stretch, but only if the
    header still reads as that form (C02 / ADULT / ASSESSMENT).
  * 5-8 vertex contours may use minAreaRect as the page quad.

Atlas: identity cells re-measured to the NPO blank value cells (four
decimals, inset). Added __display.organisation. Did not invent model fields
for the assessment Yes/No ticks.

FINDING 3 - C03 one row per field
---------------------------------
The old 3-decimal boxes were 1.5-2x too tall and sat on the wrong rows
(CYCW name / Beneficiary ID), which is why OCR returned 'nAPio' / 'Khany'
for Mpilo Khanyi. Re-measured left-column value cells on c03_p0.png to four
decimals, one row each. Added __display.cycw_name. Targets remain
caregiver.* / household.org_household_number.

FINDING 4 - member.N handwrite ruling lines
-------------------------------------------
_c01_member now insets name, surname, known_as, nationality, describe, rel,
and dates. Page-0 "Relationship to Member 1-4" boxes are inset the same way.
The exact Phase 4 fragments ('WE Poe ge', 'eR', 'eae', '_ > Fee Oe eee |')
do not come back.

FINDING 5 - CW 05 checkboxes on printed words
---------------------------------------------
The seven boxes that sat on "No", "Yes", "Open file", "Emergency", "High",
"Mild" were re-measured onto the empty squares on intake_p1.png / intake_p2.png.
Production reading still subtracts the blank, so a square's own outline is
not a tick. Drawn ticks on those squares read as ticked.

FINDING 6 - 12-digit SA ID crops
-------------------------------
member.0's 13th grid line was the crop's right edge. All four member ID
strips and the caregiver strip are now the 13-cell outer bounds plus a
right pad, inset off the ruling, and they no longer run into the passport
grid on the right.
