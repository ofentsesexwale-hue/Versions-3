================================================================================
1. EMPTY / RULING-NOISE CELLS
================================================================================

Known As and Describe stay blank when the crop has almost no ink beyond the
printed table rules (ink gate). Ruling-line removal and smash rules blank
"te re ee" / "eee" style noise and table chrome such as "IVember 4.".


================================================================================
2. CLOSED VOCAB MUST SURVIVE CLEANUP
================================================================================

Bug: member nationality OCR near-misses were matched to "South African", then
_clean_fields re-sanitized and blanked the value because "South African" is
also a printed form label.

Fix: sanitize / vocab keep closed-list canonicals; _clean_fields does not
re-sanitize a field that already has vocab_match + value.


================================================================================
3. NAME READING
================================================================================

* Split glued CamelCase OCR ("SisiLebtie" → "Sisi Lebtie").
* Ensemble of plain + ruling-stripped + CLAHE preps; keep the best non-gibberish.
* Title-case after sanitize.


================================================================================
4. SA ID DIGITS
================================================================================

* Extra 2× upscale prep (often recovers 8 that plain prep reads as 6).
* Unique single-digit confusable repair with DOB digits frozen (first six).
* Ambiguous repairs left for staff Confirm — never invent an ID.
* best_sa_id_reading prefers a variant that becomes valid after that repair.

Example: 2407150602085 → 2407150802085 (Moholoza member 1).


================================================================================
5. GEO / RELATIONSHIP HEURISTICS
================================================================================

* "Wesb Daina" → West Rand.
* Stronger South African / Grandchild near-miss shapes.
* Grandmother requires "moth" (not bare "mot") to avoid false matches.
