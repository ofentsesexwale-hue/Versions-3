# Word templates are the official originals

The NPO PDF (`NPO_case_management_file.pdf`) is a **guide** for how a household
file is assembled. Do **not** write, print, or scan by overlaying that PDF.

Fill these **Word** documents (and the `.doc` CW forms). The current product
still stamps compiled NPO PNG blanks (`ATLAS_VERSION` `npo-pdf-v1.3`) until
print/scan is rebuilt onto native Word.

Sheet-by-sheet field lists below were read from the Word XML in
`docs/official/dsd-source/`.

## C01 — Household / family details

From `CCG_Form_C01.docx` (C01: Household Details, CCG Form, v.1.2).

- Org Household Nr., House Number, Street, Town, Personnel.
- Province, District, Municipality, Ward (empty on the blank — no gazetteer).
- Head of household: type of ID (SA ID / passport / permit), 13-cell ID,
  organisation beneficiary number, headship (parent / grandparent / youth /
  child / relative / other), name, surname, known as, nationality, DOB, sex,
  race, marital status, disability, cell, home language, date joined,
  relationship to members 1–4.
- Members 1–4: same identity block; relationship to head. Note on the form:
  complete an extra form for 5 members and higher.
- Date captured / data capturer / date validated / quality assurer.

Scan atlas today covers identity + geography. Closed-vocab match remains the
three names attested on the handwritten C01 photo (Gauteng, West Rand,
Westonaria). Word cells are empty — do not invent South African place names.

last day from school or crèche; School uniform needed; School fees /

## DSD HIV Risk Assessment full pack

`DSD_HIV_Risk_Assessment_FULL_PACK.docx` is **several templates**, not one
form. Word sections: portrait, then landscape, then portrait.

Household member HIV fields already in the app (`hiv_status`, `on_art`,
`last_viral_load`, `hiv_test_date`, `hiv_risk_notes`) correspond to this pack.
There is **no atlas** yet.

### 1. HIV RISK ASSESSMENT FORM

Header: Organisation/Service Point; Job Title; Parent's/Legal
Guardian/Caregiver Full Name and Contact Details; Child's Full Name and
Contact Details; Child's ID Number/Date of Birth; Date of assessment; Gender;
Race; Disability (Y/N).

**Part 1 — HIV screening**

- Is the child's HIV status known (Yes / No).
- What is the status: Positive / Negative / Unknown.
- **Positive:** stop the risk assessment; enrol in treatment and care;
  support adherence and retention.
- **Negative:** if the test is more than 6 months ago and the child is over 13
  and sexually active, continue to Part 2. If tested within the last 6 months,
  HIV test is not needed (HTS Policy); stop and refer to social and behaviour
  change programmes.
- **Unknown:** establish why status is unknown and whether a risk assessment
  or HIV test was ever done. If neither, proceed to Part 2.

**Part 2 — Risk indicators** (Yes / No)

Circumstantial (parental/family):

- Are either of the child's biological parents reported to be HIV positive.
- Is one or both parents deceased due to a known HIV related illness.
- Does the child have one or more siblings that are deceased.
- Is any member of the household addicted to substances.
- Have there been incidents of domestic violence in the family.

Any Yes → child is at risk; supportive referral for testing is advised
(consent first).

Health indicators (SSP observes / enquires; Road to Health card where
available):

- Sick often, or sick for more than three weeks (fever, weight loss, runny
  stomach, swollen lymph nodes, yeast infections, diarrhoea, ear infection,
  skin problems, lung infections, bacterial infections, mouth ulcers).
- Small and/or underweight for age; does not gain weight on regular meals.
- Diagnosed with TB.
- Admitted to hospital in the past six months.

Behavioural — younger child (ask the primary caregiver):

- Suspect exposure to sexual violence (mandatory report to police or social
  worker).
- Know if the child has been sexually abused.

Behavioural — adolescent/youth interview:

- Intimate partner; age difference; sexually active; sexual activity with
  anyone other than the intimate partner; sexually and/or physically abused;
  drunk or high and had sex, or suspect abuse while intoxicated.

**Part 3 — Assessment result and action**

- Test Required / Test Not Required / Routine testing every six months
  required.
- Child referred for HIV testing (health facility).
- Date for follow-up; confirmation of HIV testing received.
- Enter referrals on the Referral Log and the child's file.
- Social Service Practitioner overall comments and concerns.

### 2. Primary Caregiver Consent to Supportive Referral for HIV Testing

- Caregiver name; children 1–3 with dates of birth; residential area.
- Grant permission for referral for HIV testing.
- YES / NO initials or fingerprint on: informed of nature/benefits/risks;
  freely agree to referral; results in person and follow-up; option to
  disclose results; option to withhold disclosure; free to withdraw consent.
- Caregiver ID; SSP SACSSP number; address; service point / organisation;
  signed at / date / signature / print name / mobile; date stamp required.

### 3. Child Assent Form for referral for HIV Testing

Same YES / NO block for the child/adolescent/youth (identity number, SSP
SACSSP, signatures, date stamp).

### 5–6. HTS Tracking Form / Beneficiary Details

- First name, surname, DOB (D D M M Y Y Y Y), age, sex, ID number.
- Confidential: is the status of this beneficiary known (YES / NO).
- Disclosed status: Positive / Negative / Unknown (offer HIV test or referral).
- On ART: Yes / No (if No, refer for ART). Confirmation of ART referral;
  date of completion; date of most recent ART initiation; last CD4 date and
  count; last viral load date and band (≥1000 not suppressed; ≥50 and <1000
  suppressed but detectable; <50 undetectable/LDL); facility where ART is
  received.
- If Negative: risk assessment every 12 months for beneficiaries over 13.
- Why status unknown: never tested; refuse to disclose whether they have ever
  tested; refuse to disclose HIV status; other.
- Has this child been risk assessed; outcome (test not indicated / test
  required); referral date and clinic name.
- Personnel / data capture / supervisor / data validation names, signatures,
  dates.
